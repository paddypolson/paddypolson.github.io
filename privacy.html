claude-tracker privacy policy

claude-tracker is a private tool used only by its developer, with their own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): recent messages are read to find bills, receipts, deliveries, bookings and requested actions. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Calendar: it creates one calendar and adds, updates or removes events in that calendar only. It has read-only access to the owner's other calendars, used to notice events added to a calendar shared with the owner's household and to avoid duplicates and clashes. It cannot change any other calendar.

How data is used and stored

  • Extracted details (dates, amounts, vendors, event details) are stored on the owner's own home server and shown only to the owner.
  • Email text is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service solely to extract those details. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone else.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
